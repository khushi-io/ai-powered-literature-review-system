Help & guide

Everything you need to read the results.
How do I start?
Open “New review”, type a topic and press “Run review”. Options are on the same screen.
What is the quality score?
A number from 0 to 1 that averages four checks: coverage (how much of the papers the summary uses), specificity (how concrete the research ideas are), support (how many sentences the sources back up) and novelty (how few ideas already exist). 0.75 or more is strong.
What does “claims verified” mean?
Every summary sentence was compared with the papers by a language-inference model. Sentences the papers do not support are removed.
Is a “no close match found” idea really new?
Not necessarily. Only the OpenAlex library is searched, so please still check the literature yourself.
Where are my reports?
In the “exports” folder of the project after pressing Export Word or Export PDF.
Is anything sent to an AI service?
No. All analysis runs on your computer; only paper searches go to OpenAlex.
